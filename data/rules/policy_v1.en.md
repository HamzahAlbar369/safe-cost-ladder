# Oasis Motor Insurance — Motor Policy Wording (v1)

> FICTIONAL INSURER. Rules follow the SAMA Unified Compulsory Motor Insurance Policy and Comprehensive Motor Insurance Rules (articles cited), administered by the Insurance Authority; insurer-specific terms and the insurer are illustrative. Not legal advice.

## 1. How claims are decided

- Every claim is either an own-damage claim (the policyholder's own vehicle) or a third-party claim (another party claims against the policyholder).
- Approve: the claim is payable and is reduced only by the deductible, if any.
- Partial: the claim is payable but reduced by the fault share or by a cap (the sum insured or the third-party limit).
- Deny: nothing is paid (out of scope, policy not in force, exclusion, no own-damage cover, late report, 0% fault, or loss at or below the deductible).
- Need info: nothing is paid yet because a required document is missing or two stated values contradict each other.
- All amounts are in Saudi riyals (SAR), rounded half up to a whole riyal.

## 2. What this claims desk does not handle

- This desk handles vehicle collision damage only. Claims for the following are outside these rules and are denied here; the customer is referred to the relevant team:
  - bodily injury or death
  - theft of the vehicle
  - fire
  - flood

## 3. Cover types and policy period

- Third-Party Liability (TPL) covers the policyholder's liability for damage to other people's vehicles and property. It does not cover the policyholder's own vehicle; an own-damage claim under TPL is denied.
- Comprehensive (COMP) covers the policyholder's own vehicle under section 7 and third-party claims under section 5.
- If the accident happened after the policy end date, the policy was not in force and the claim is denied. A policy is in force up to and including its end date.

## 4. Fault share

- The policyholder's fault share is taken from the accident report (Najm or the traffic police) and is one of 0, 25, 50, 75 or 100 percent.

## 5. Third-party claims against the policyholder

- Payout = the third party's damage × the policyholder's fault share, up to SAR 10,000,000 per accident. This applies under TPL and COMP.
- If the policyholder's fault share is 0%, nothing is payable and the claim is denied.

## 6. Third-party claims: exclusions and right of recovery

- A third-party claim is denied if any of the following applies:
  - the driver fled the accident scene
  - driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  - drifting (tafheet) or racing
  - the accident was caused deliberately
  - material misrepresentation in the claim
- A third-party claim is still paid under section 5 if any of the following applies, and the insurer keeps a right to recover the amount from the policyholder or the driver:
  - the driver had no valid licence (an expired licence renewed within the grace period counts as valid)
  - driving against the direction of traffic
  - crossing a red light
  - using the vehicle for a purpose other than the one in the policy
- For this section, a licence that had expired on the accident date counts as valid if it was renewed within 50 days after the accident.

## 7. Own-damage claims (COMP only)

- Total loss: if the assessment report declares a technical total loss, or the repair estimate is above 60% of the sum insured, the base amount is the sum insured. Otherwise the base amount is the repair estimate, capped at the sum insured.
- Nothing is deducted for the vehicle's prior use or wear.
- The deductible is SAR 1,000, as stated in the schedule, and stays fixed for the policy period.
- Deductible applied = the deductible × the policyholder's fault share. If the policyholder is not liable (0%), no deductible is applied.
- If there is no other party (single-vehicle accident), the full deductible is applied. If the other party is unknown (hit-and-run), no deductible is applied.
- Payout = base amount − deductible applied. The deductible also applies to a total loss.
- If the repair estimate is at or below the full deductible (SAR 1,000), the claim is denied, whatever the fault share.
- If an identified, insured other party is 100% at fault, the customer may claim from that party's insurer, or this insurer pays with no deductible and recovers from the other party's insurer.

## 8. Own-damage exclusions

- An own-damage claim is not paid in any of these cases:
  - loss under an optional cover the insured declined
  - no valid licence for the vehicle class, or a withdrawn licence; an expired licence counts as valid if renewed within the grace period
  - the assessed loss is at or below the deductible
  - the vehicle was driven by someone other than the insured or an authorised driver
  - manufacturing defects or mechanical or electrical failure
  - damage to tyres, rims or mirrors alone, unless caused in a covered accident
  - loss of cargo or personal belongings in the vehicle
  - damage to a trailer not named in the schedule
  - theft from a running or unlocked vehicle
  - accessories not fitted by the manufacturer
  - use that breaks the policy's limits on use
  - carrying more passengers or load than permitted, causing the accident
  - racing, speed trials or testing
  - a driver impaired by alcohol, drugs or medicines
  - use of the vehicle as working machinery
  - drifting, crossing a red light or driving against traffic, when the report shows it caused the accident
  - use inside restricted areas such as airports or seaports
  - criminal or hostile acts by the insured or driver
  - an accident caused deliberately
  - war, terrorism, nuclear incidents or rebellion
  - driving in the desert or on unpaved roads outside cities
- Licence: an expired licence counts as valid if it was renewed within 50 business days (Sunday to Thursday) after the accident. A licence for the wrong vehicle class, or a withdrawn or suspended licence, gets no grace period.
- Authorised drivers are:
  - the insured
  - a named driver on the schedule
  - a relative of the insured
  - the insured's domestic worker
  - a driver sponsored by the insured

## 9. Unknown, uninsured or no other party

- Under COMP, own damage is paid under section 7 whatever the other party's status. For a hit-and-run there is no deductible and no recovery from another party.
- Under TPL, if the other party is unknown or uninsured, nothing covers the policyholder's own vehicle and the claim is denied.
- The accident report may come from Najm or from the traffic police, whichever attended.

## 10. Reporting window

- A claim must be reported to the insurer within 15 days of the accident. A claim reported on day 15 is on time.
- A claim reported later is denied unless the customer states one of these accepted reasons:
  - the customer was in hospital
  - the customer was travelling outside the Kingdom
  - the reporting authority delayed the accident report
- Other reasons are not accepted, for example:
  - being busy at work
  - forgetting
  - not knowing the deadline

## 11. Required documents

- Every claim needs all of the following:
  - the accident report (Najm or traffic police)
  - the vehicle registration
  - a copy of the driving licence
  - photos of the damage
  - a damage assessment report (for example Taqdeer)
- If any is missing, or if two stated values for the same item contradict each other, the decision is need info and no payout is computed.

## 12. Order of application

- Rules are checked in this order, and the first one that leads to a decision decides: out of scope (2) → policy not in force (3) → third-party exclusions (6) → own damage under TPL (3, 9) → own-damage exclusions (8) → reporting window (10) → loss at or below the deductible, when the damage assessment is present (7) → required documents (11) → payout (5 or 7).

## 13. Service standards and other benefits

- If documents are missing, the insurer tells individual customers within 3 working days.
- Claims below SAR 2,000 are settled within 5 working days; other claims within 15 days.
- A total-loss report is issued within 7 working days of complete documents.
- Claim statuses: Incomplete, Outstanding, Settled, Rejected.
- Towing is covered per claim up to SAR 500 inside a city and SAR 1,000 outside a city.
- A replacement car is not part of this policy. It is an optional add-on that needs the insurer's prior approval.
